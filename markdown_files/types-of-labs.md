# Types of Labs We Offer

VBL offers several types of labs, all located in the Transitorium building, each suited to different kinds of studies. Choose the one that best matches your study's setup and equipment needs.

>[!info] Common Lab (Computerzaal K2)
> A large computer Lab for 20 participants, which can be booked for internet-based, large-scale studies in a behavioural economics setting.

>[!info] 15 Cubicle Lab (K2B-74)
> One of two cubicle labs, with 15 individual soundproofed cubicles that can be fully closed off to minimize outside noise. This lab can be split into two, allowing two different experiments to run at the same time. Suitable for a wide variety of studies, from surveys to experiments measuring reaction times, and can be used for solitary work or for experiments in which participants collaborate with or compete against each other. Includes an experimenter desk, and each cubicle has a desktop computer, keyboard, mouse, webcam, and audio headset.

>[!info] 11 Cubicle Lab (K2-E17)
> One of two cubicle labs, with 11 individual soundproofed cubicles that can be fully closed off to minimize outside noise. Suitable for a wide variety of studies, from surveys to experiments measuring reaction times, and can be used for solitary work or for experiments in which participants collaborate with or compete against each other. Includes an experimenter desk, and each cubicle has a desktop computer, keyboard, mouse, webcam, and audio headset.

>[!info] Small Group Lab (K2-D74 & K2-D72)
> Two identical lab spaces next to each other, which can be flexibly set up. Suitable for in-depth interviews, eye-tracking studies, behavioural intervention studies (e.g., clean versus messy, two different supermarket settings, warm versus cold), or smaller group settings such as discussions or negotiations. Lab dividers, tables (14 small, 6 large), chairs (20), and cameras (2) are available upon request.

>[!info] Large Group Lab (K2E-41)
> A spacious Lab for 1–15 persons, ideal for group observations such as focus groups, team observations, and leadership observations. Lab dividers, tables (14 small, 6 large), chairs (20), and cameras (2) are available upon request to flexibly organize the Lab.


## Other Available Facilities

- **Lockers**
- **Eye Tracker (EyeLink 1000 Desktop Mount)** — tracks eye movements at up to 1000 Hz with one eye, allowing comprehensive analysis of eye movement characteristics such as fixation time, frequency, and the length and direction of saccades as a direct function of the experimental task.

Once you've identified the Lab type that fits your study, check the calendar for open slots.
