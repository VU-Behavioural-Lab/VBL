
# Step 1: Types of Labs and Availability

Before setting up your study on SONA, you'll need to check which lab room type suits your study and confirm a suitable timeslot is available.

This step will walk you through:

- The different types of labs available at VBL
- Check the availability of Labs
- The rules for booking a Lab

Once you've identified a suitable Lab and timeslot, you can move on to creating your study in SONA.
