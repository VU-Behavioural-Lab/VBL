# Rules for Booking a Lab

Once you've found a suitable Lab and timeslot on the calendar, keep the following rules in mind:

1. **Book in advance.** The 15 Cubicle Lab can only be booked up to 1 month in advance. All other Labs have no advance booking time restriction.
2. **Maximum booking duration.** The 15 Cubicle Lab can only be booked for a maximum of 1 month at a time. All other Labs have no maximum duration limit.
3. **Cancellations.** If you need to cancel or reschedule, do so at least 1 week in advance so the slot can be released to others.
4. **Lab usage.** Leave the Lab as you found it and report any equipment issues to the lab coordinator immediately.
5. **Faculty specific rules.** SBE may use the labs on average 3 days/week, i.e. up to 156 days/year. All other faculties may use the lab on average 2 days/week, i.e. up to 104 days/year.

>[!warning] Availability on the calendar is not a confirmed booking
> An open slot on the calendar means the Lab is likely free, but your reservation isn't final until it's been confirmed through the lab coordinator.

If you have questions about booking policies, contact vbl@vu.nl .
